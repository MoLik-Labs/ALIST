# Kova product context

Kova is a self-hosted file workspace for browsing and managing storage across
local disks and cloud providers. People use it repeatedly to locate, preview,
share and organize files, and administrators configure mounts, accounts and
permissions. Familiar file-manager interaction, clear authorization and
recoverable failures matter more than decorative novelty.

The production server and existing data remain independent of the imported
AList Go source. Do not imply driver parity, provider compatibility or durable
Render Free storage without tests. Interfaces ship in English and Vietnamese,
with light and dark modes, keyboard access, reduced motion and narrow-screen
support. Runtime assets are self-hosted and the application must remain useful
without external fonts, analytics or CDNs.
